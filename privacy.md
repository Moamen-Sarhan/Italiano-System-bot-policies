# Privacy Policy — Italiano Company Bot

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Italiano Company Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

The Bot collects and stores the following:

### Automatically Collected
- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs
- Discord Channel IDs
- Discord Role IDs
- Message timestamps

### User-Provided
- Application answers (name, age, experience, etc.)
- Farm rental details
- Skin purchase proofs (image URLs)
- Support ticket content
- Character names and IDs

### Not Collected
- We do NOT collect passwords
- We do NOT collect payment information
- We do NOT read private messages
- We do NOT collect IP addresses

## 3. How We Use Information

Data is used strictly for:
- Processing applications
- Managing farm rentals
- Processing skin purchases
- Handling support tickets
- Moderation purposes

## 4. Data Storage

Data is stored securely using SQLite databases on our hosting server. We retain data only as long as necessary for the Bot's operation.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your data with third parties, except:
- When required by law
- To protect our rights or safety

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request deletion of your data
- Request a copy of your stored data
- Opt out of data collection by not using the Bot

To exercise these rights, open a support ticket in our Discord server.

## 7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures, but no system is 100% secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country).

## 9. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the Bot means acceptance of changes.

## 10. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us through our Discord server.
